What am I seeing?

Each coloured vehicle is one simulated robot. A gold square/diamond marks the selected robot's current goal; a gold ring marks a recorded task completion. A wait means the robot stayed in its cell for one discrete transition. A backward move increases unweighted distance to its goal active before movement. Displayed-step fleet physical delay sums one unit per wait and two per backward move over all robots; it is not throughput. The goal displayed at a completion step may already be the newly assigned goal. The 2.5D heading and short trail are derived from grid positions, not physical orientation or a safety model.

What this comparison establishes

The two runs share the same initial positions, synthetic million-entry task pool, task RNG, planner RNG and priority-order RNG. Their planner actions diverge after guidance is applied; later physical states, goal-reset times and exposed tasks can differ. The maps and task streams are simulation inputs, not warehouse operational records.

Throughput uses completed tasks / 400 scored transitions (201–600). The current-frame physical cost and delay heat are diagnostic quantities, not throughput.
