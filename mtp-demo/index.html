This public site replays retained simulation traces. It does not run new simulations, accept operational data, or provide physical robot control.

What the replay makes visible

Movement and waiting

Follow a robot, its active goal and its executed trail. Backward movement is measured against unweighted distance to the goal active before movement.

Hidden planning work

Inspect a recorded recursive event. Attempted calls are planning computation, while only the committed joint action moves the robots.

Matched inputs, different futures

The two arms share starts and a latent synthetic task stream. Their own-policy trajectories and later task consumption can diverge.

Existing fleet

Compare declared fleet counts under the same workload. Report the best observed configuration in that menu, not a universal optimum.

Proposed layout

Compare admissible edits against an unchanged baseline with paired episode seeds. A hotspot is a reason to investigate, not proof that an edit helps.

Results are tasks per simulated transition. No real warehouse order data, physical safety certification, energy saving or financial saving is established by this demonstration.

Methods and provenance

PIBT and Guidance Graph Optimization are existing methods. Guidance performance is credited to their original authors. This demonstration adds execution replay and diagnostic presentation; it does not introduce a new optimizer.

PIBT: Okumura et al., Artificial Intelligence 310 (2022), 103752. Paper.
Guidance Graph Optimization: Zhang et al., IJCAI 2024, pp. 311–320. Paper · Official artifact.

The 33 by 36 warehouse-style map and fixed PIBT/400-agent field originate from the GGO public artifact, revision d8de695dbd95c912ee59e8e626d63e511da684a7. Map rows are unchanged; tasks are synthetic. The upstream README licenses its code as MIT except RHCR; a separate map-data licence is not stated. The replay records are retained outputs of the project simulator, not an upstream benchmark score.
